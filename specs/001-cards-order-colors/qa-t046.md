## Pill pixels (SC-003, SC-004)

Thirteen cards (the twelve presets + one custom hex) seeded into IndexedDB, rendered in
both Playwright projects and both themes, each chip screenshotted on its own and the PNG
scanned: the pill background is sampled inside the rounded cap, and the label is the pixel
in the text band with the greatest contrast against that background (the glyph core).

| Card colour | Preset | Label | Measured ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| `#DC2626` | Tomato | white | **4.83** |
| `#EA580C` | Orange | dark | **5.02** |
| `#D97706` | Amber | dark | **5.60** |
| `#CA8A04` | Banana | dark | **6.08** |
| `#65A30D` | Lime | dark | **5.78** |
| `#16A34A` | Basil | dark | **5.42** |
| `#0D9488` | Teal | dark | **4.77** |
| `#0C74B0` | Sky | white | **5.07** |
| `#2563EB` | Blueberry | white | **5.17** |
| `#7C3AED` | Grape | white | **5.70** |
| `#C026D3` | Fuchsia | white | **4.71** |
| `#DB2777` | Flamingo | white | **4.60** |
| `#8899AA` | (custom pick) | dark | **6.11** |

- Every pill paints its **exact** hex — no blend. This is what the `opacity-90` removal
  bought: at 90% the blueberry pill measured `#3B73ED` and its label 4.32:1, under AA.
- Every label clears 4.5:1; the tightest are Flamingo 4.60, Fuchsia 4.71 and Teal 4.77.
- The six flips FR-010b predicted are visible in the Label column: orange, amber, banana,
  lime, basil and teal now carry the dark label.
- Identical in light and dark theme — a card's colour is the card's, not the theme's.
- Chip height: **44px** at phone width (`min-h-[44px]`), 34px from `sm:` up.

## A reorder fires no Calendar traffic (FR-007)

Same seed, network log filtered to `googleapis.com/calendar` across a full drag: the row
went `C0,C1,C2,…` → `C1,C2,C0,…` and the log was **empty** on both projects. A rank change
is one Dexie row write plus one `pushDataJson`; `bulkUpdateCardEvents` is reachable only
from a colour change.

## Observation, out of scope

The de-emphasised text inside a calendar entry chip (the time, the icons) carries
`opacity-80`, which blends it into the card colour: 3.40–4.58:1 across the presets, under
AA. It is **pre-existing** and this feature improved it rather than caused it — with the
old always-white label the same text measured 2.42–4.22:1, and the six label flips lifted
their chips (e.g. amber 2.57 → 4.34). Fixing the remaining gap means deciding how
de-emphasised that text should be, which is an appearance change of its own; SC-004 is
about the card pill label and is met.
